# CART-TRACE Governed Results Manuscript Placeholder

## Status

Template only. No governed patient data or empirical clinical results are represented in this public artifact.

## Purpose

Provide manuscript-ready placeholders for an approved governed-data application while preventing synthetic results, methodological readiness, and external clinical validity from being conflated.

## Cohort and source availability

Report:

- candidate therapy episodes;
- valid infusion anchors;
- source-domain availability;
- final reconstructability classes;
- reasons for exclusion or non-reconstructability;
- metric-specific denominator availability.

## Mapping and data quality

Report:

- local mapping version and review status;
- mapping coverage;
- unmapped/ambiguous label burden;
- timestamp quality;
- overlap/conflict/open-end burden;
- provenance coverage.

## Representation validation

Report the prespecified validation-sample size and aggregate concordance for infusion anchor, encounter boundaries, care-state mapping, transition timing, discharge/return interpretation, and frozen metric outputs. Summarize discrepancy categories and resolutions without exposing patient-level adjudication evidence.

## Descriptive utilization results

If governed execution is authorized and completed, report only frozen CART-TRACE outputs such as state-duration distributions, escalation/de-escalation transitions, discharge timing, acute-care return measures, uncertainty burden, and metric availability. Do not infer toxicity severity, treatment appropriateness, or causal effects from care location.

## Required limitations language

The Results/Discussion must distinguish:

1. synthetic computational validity;
2. governed representation fidelity;
3. descriptive empirical findings;
4. limits of source completeness and mapping;
5. the absence of prospective decision-support validation;
6. the fact that level of care is not a direct measure of toxicity or physiologic severity;
7. any selection introduced by reconstructability or follow-up requirements.

## Disclosure rule

No governed aggregate result enters the public repository or manuscript package until disclosure review permits release. If governed access is unavailable, this placeholder remains unpopulated and the manuscript explicitly describes the planned validation design rather than presenting simulated empirical findings as observed data.
